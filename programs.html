Our Programs

Three powerful ways we restore the planet and empower communities

At GreenRoots, we believe lasting change comes from combining action on the ground with knowledge and community ownership. That’s why our work is built around three core programs.

Program 01

Tree Planting

We restore degraded lands by planting native trees that belong to the local ecosystem. Every project is carefully planned with community input to ensure the right trees are planted in the right places.

  • Native species selection
  • Community-led planting days
  • Long-term forest monitoring
  • Soil restoration techniques
Join a Planting Day
Program 02

Climate Education

Knowledge creates lasting change. We run workshops, school programs, and training sessions that help people understand climate change and take practical action in their own communities.

  • School climate clubs
  • Community workshops
  • Teacher training programs
  • Youth leadership camps
Support Education
Program 03

Community Projects

We partner with local groups to create green livelihoods. From tree nurseries and beekeeping to sustainable farming, these projects help communities protect nature while improving their income.

  • Community tree nurseries
  • Green livelihood training
  • Women-led environmental groups
  • Local conservation enterprises
