Stories of Change

Behind every number is a community transformed

Forest Restoration

Reviving the Hillside Forests

In partnership with local farmers, we planted over 420,000 native trees across eroded hillsides. Within three years, water sources returned and crop yields improved.

Climate Education

Green Schools Network

We established climate clubs in 95 schools. Students have led local clean-ups, started tree nurseries, and educated their families on sustainable practices.

Community Livelihoods

Women-Led Tree Nurseries

Twelve community nurseries run by women’s groups now produce over 300,000 seedlings every year, creating income while supporting reforestation efforts.

Carbon Impact

Our forests are estimated to capture more than 85,000 tons of CO₂ annually as they mature.

Biodiversity

Restored areas have seen the return of native birds, insects, and small mammals that had disappeared from the region.

Community Benefits

Improved water retention, reduced soil erosion, and new green income sources for hundreds of families.
