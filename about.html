About GreenRoots

Restoring the planet through trees, education, and community action

Our Story

GreenRoots Initiative started in 2020 when a small group of environmentalists, teachers, and community leaders came together with one simple belief: restoring forests can transform both the land and people’s lives.

What began as a local tree-planting effort has grown into a movement that has planted over 2.4 million trees, reached more than 180 communities, and inspired thousands of people to take climate action.

Today, we work hand-in-hand with local communities, schools, and partners to restore degraded land, teach climate solutions, and create greener, more resilient futures.

Founder & Director

Lugaaya Mathew

Lugaaya Mathew is the Founder and Director of GreenRoots Initiative. Passionate about environmental conservation and community development, he started the organization with a clear vision: to restore degraded lands through tree planting while empowering local communities with climate education.

Driven by the belief that real change begins at the grassroots level, Lugaaya has led efforts to plant millions of trees, establish community nurseries, and create educational programs in schools. Under his leadership, GreenRoots has grown into a movement that combines environmental restoration with sustainable livelihoods.

He continues to work closely with communities, partners, and volunteers to build a greener and more resilient future.

Our Mission

To restore degraded ecosystems through large-scale tree planting, empower communities with climate education, and build a sustainable future for generations to come.

Our Vision

A world where forests thrive, communities are climate-resilient, and every person has the knowledge and opportunity to protect the planet.
